Indicator Super Trend Pullback Signals for Trading View
 
Super trend Pullback Signals is a trend-following continuation indicator designed to identify structured pullbacks within established trends.

The script combines Super trend detection with ATR-based pullback analysis, Dynamic Value Zones, and momentum confirmation to highlight areas where trend continuation may be developing after a retracement.
 
Important Features:

Built-in Supertrend trend detection
Dynamic Value Zone around the Supertrend line
ATR-based Pullback Quality Filter (PQF)
Momentum confirmation candle requirement
One signal per trend leg
Bullish and Bearish continuation signals
Trend flip markers
Alert support
Clean chart visualization
