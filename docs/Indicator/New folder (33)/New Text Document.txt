Indicator CTZ Swing Exhaustion Levels for Trading View 

Where does this swing end, and when? Statistical swing targets + kernel trend, tuned for crypto.

Most indicators tell you a swing has ended after it's over. This one estimates WHERE and WHEN the live swing is likely to end, using the chart's own history of past swings.

It tracks every swing on your chart (measured in ATR), learns how far and how long swings of this size usually run, and projects:

• 3 exhaustion levels (50% / 75% / 90%)
• A time window for when the swing is likely to finish
• Live odds that the top or bottom is already in
• A kernel regression trend, so you know whether the swing is moving WITH or AGAINST the trend
